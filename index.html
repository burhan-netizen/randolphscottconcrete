Questions

Good to know.

Still have a question? Call (919) 521-7384 or email rsconcrete407@gmail.com.

Are estimates really free?

Yes. We come out, measure the area, talk through your options and give you a written price. You are never under any obligation.

How soon can I walk or drive on new concrete?

It depends on the mix, the slab and the weather. As a general guide, foot traffic comes days before vehicles. We will give you exact timing for your project when we finish.

Can you extend my existing patio?

Often, yes. We look at the condition and level of your current slab during the estimate and tell you honestly whether extending or replacing makes more sense.

What finishes can I choose from?

Broom, smooth and edged finishes, curved and framed borders, diamond scoring and stamped or textured panels. See the finishes section above for real examples.

Do you handle small jobs?

Absolutely. AC pads, trash can pads, short walkways and small repairs get the same care as a full patio or slab.

What about rain?

Florida weather is part of the plan. We schedule pours around the forecast and protect fresh concrete when a storm rolls in.
